Before you arrive

Prepare for
your journey.

Bali visa

Indonesian tourist visas allow a 30-day stay. Our retreat runs 15 days, so most guests are comfortably within limits. Confirm your specific passport requirements with your country's Indonesian embassy before booking.

Transportation

Airport transfers to and from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) are included in your retreat cost, as is all transport to and from planned group excursions — temples, waterfalls, and any shared outings.

Health & vaccinations

Consult your doctor 6–8 weeks before travel. Hepatitis A and Typhoid are commonly recommended for Bali. Travel insurance that includes medical evacuation coverage is strongly advised for all guests.

What to pack

Light breathable clothing, a sarong for temple visits, reef-safe sunscreen, and a yoga mat strap. April is Bali's transition from wet to dry season — bring light layers and a rain jacket for evenings.

Money & spending

All meals and group excursions are included in your retreat cost. Bring Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) for personal shopping, tips, and any optional activities. ATMs are widely available throughout Canggu.

Connectivity

The villa has reliable WiFi. A local SIM card (Telkomsel or XL) is inexpensive and available at the airport on arrival. We encourage a mindful relationship with screens during retreat time.
