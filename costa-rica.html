Before You Go

What you need
to know.

✈️

Airport

Fly into Juan Santamaría International (SJO) in San José. A shared shuttle brings you to the retreat center (~2 hrs).

🛂

Entry Visa

US and most EU citizens don't need a visa for stays under 90 days — just a valid passport (6+ months validity) and a return ticket.

💉

Vaccinations

No vaccinations legally required. CDC recommends Hepatitis A & B, Typhoid. Speak with your doctor about additional precautions.

🎒

What to Pack

Light yoga wear, a rain jacket, reef-safe sunscreen, insect repellent, personal medications. Full packing list sent after registration.
