Who We Are

Practice rooted in place

Aethera was born from a simple belief: that the most profound transformations happen when movement meets meaning. We bring small groups to places of extraordinary energy: the cloud forests of Costa Rica, the Himalayan highlands of Nepal, the sacred river valleys of India.
